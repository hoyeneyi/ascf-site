The Festival

Three days of music, art, food, and competition in downtown Pontiac — built to do something more than entertain.

What it is

America’s Spring Canvas Festival runs Memorial Day Weekend 2028 across downtown Pontiac, Michigan. Music on multiple stages, a working artist market, food from local kitchens, a family block, and a rock paper scissors world championship with real prize money.

The programming is built around one idea: conflict is easiest to resolve between people who have already enjoyed something together.
