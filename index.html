Why this festival exists

A conflict resolution
campaign you can
dance at.

Spring Canvas is built around a single message: disagreements can end before they turn into violence. The festival carries that message to the people least likely to sit through a lecture about it.

Alongside the four days in May, the campaign runs in the community all year — billboards, a print campaign, radio, and an awards banquet honoring students who resolved conflict peacefully.

What's on the grounds

Three ways in.

Every corner of the festival is built around the same idea — that the differences between us are worth showing off rather than fighting over.

eATERIES

Fifty local restaurants and food trucks serving the food of the communities that make up this country.

eXHIBITS

A hundred and fifty artists — painting, glasswork, jewelry, print — showing and selling across four days.

eNTERTAINMENT

Eight stages running every day, from local acts in the afternoon to national headliners at night.

Scissors.
