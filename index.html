Why this festival exists

Pontiac has been divided along lines nobody chose — block by block, school by school. We think the fastest way past that isn't another panel discussion. It's giving people a reason to stand next to each other for three days and enjoy the same thing.

Play beats debate

The RPS tournament puts strangers across from each other in a game with no advantage, no history, and a handshake at the end.

Money stays here

Vendor slots are reserved for local makers and food businesses first, so the weekend leaves something behind.

Everyone gets in

Tiered pricing and a free family block on Sunday keep the gate from being the thing that divides people.
