Vendors & Artists

Booth space in front of a projected 30,000 people across three days. Local makers and kitchens get first pick.

Who we're looking for

Food, art, apparel, and craft. If you make something and you want it in front of a crowd that came out specifically to spend a weekend downtown, this is the room.

Food

Full kitchens, trucks, and stands. Health permits handled through the festival.

Artists

Original work, prints, and live demonstration booths in the artist market.

Retail

Apparel, craft, and small-batch goods.

Community

Nonprofits and local organizations. Reduced rate available.
